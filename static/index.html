Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about ClipPods.

How long can my source videos be?

On our Starter plan, you can process videos up to 1 hour long. The Creator plan supports uploads up to 2 hours. Our pipeline is optimized to extract clips efficiently regardless of length.

Do captions come automatically with the clips?

Yes, all clips are automatically transcribed using high-accuracy speech-to-text models. Captions are timed precisely to the audio and formatted in a premium style suited for mobile feeds.

Can I export directly to Shorts, Reels, and TikTok?

Absolutely. The generated clips are saved as 9:16 vertical videos (1080x1920) which is the standard format for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.

What file formats are supported?

For uploads, we support MP4, MOV, WEBM, and most other common video formats. For URL links, you can paste any public YouTube video or direct video file URL.
